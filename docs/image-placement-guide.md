**Unified Guide: How to Use Free Stock Photos & Illustrations Effectively on Websites**  
(For creators relying on Pexels, Vecteezy, undraw.co & freewebillustrations.com)

Even without original photography, you can create a professional, cohesive, and high-converting website using free stock resources. The key is **strategic selection, strong customization, and visual consistency**.

### Core Principles When Using Only Free Stock
- **Relevance & Brand Fit** > Everything else.
- **Consistency is King**: Make all visuals feel like they belong to the **same brand family**.
- **Mix Smartly**: Combine photos + illustrations for interest and uniqueness.
- **Customization Turns Generic into Custom**: This is your biggest advantage.

### Best Practices Tailored to Your Resources

#### 1. Define Your Visual Brand Style First
Before searching for any image:
- Choose 2–3 brand adjectives (e.g., modern, warm, trustworthy, energetic, minimalist).
- Pick a consistent color palette (use brand colors as overlays/filters).
- Decide on overall style:  
  – Realistic photos (Pexels + Vecteezy)  
  – Flat / line illustrations (undraw.co)  
  – Detailed illustrations (freewebillustrations.com)

**Recommended Approach**: Use **photos for emotional connection** (heroes, testimonials) and **illustrations for concepts, features, and icons**.

#### 2. Sourcing Strategy from Your Tools
| Source                  | Best Used For                     | Tips |
|-------------------------|-----------------------------------|------|
| **Pexels**              | Hero images, lifestyle, people    | Search by mood + diversity. Download highest resolution. |
| **Vecteezy**            | Photos, vectors, icons            | Great for editable vectors. |
| **undraw.co**           | Feature illustrations, icons      | Customize colors directly on site before download. |
| **freewebillustrations.com** | Detailed scenes & illustrations | Good for storytelling visuals. |

#### 3. Customization Techniques (Make Free Stock Look Premium)
- **Color Harmonization**: Apply the same color filter or overlay to all photos (e.g., warm tone or brand blue tint).
- **undraw Advantage**: Change colors to match your brand palette before downloading.
- **Photo Editing** (free tools: Canva, Photopea, GIMP, or CapCut):
  - Crop and reframe for better composition.
  - Add subtle brand overlays, gradients, or text.
  - Convert some photos into duotone or illustrated style.
  - Remove or replace backgrounds (remove.bg + Photopea).
- **Create Consistency**:
  - Use the same photo treatment across the entire site.
  - Maintain similar lighting direction and mood.
  - Create a small “image library” of 10–15 edited assets you reuse in variations.

#### 4. Recommended Page Structure & Image Usage
- **Hero Section**: One strong, relevant Pexels photo + bold overlay text. Or a large customized undraw illustration for a cleaner/modern feel.
- **Benefits / Features**: undraw.co or freewebillustrations icons + short descriptions.
- **Social Proof**: Use people photos from Pexels (diverse, natural-looking) with testimonials.
- **Product / Service Showcase**: Screenshots + illustrated explanations.
- **About Page**: One good “team” or “person working” photo from Pexels (or illustrated version if you want to avoid generic faces).

#### 5. Technical & Optimization Best Practices
- Always compress images (use TinyPNG or Squoosh).
- Prefer **WebP** format when possible.
- Use descriptive filenames and proper alt text.
- Keep hero images under 200–300 KB if possible.
- Make sure everything is fully responsive.

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Using obviously generic or mismatched stock photos.
- No color consistency (rainbow effect across the site).
- Too many different illustration styles mixed together.
- Overcrowding pages with too many images.
- Using low-resolution files.

### Pro Tips for Better Results
- Create **custom composites** (e.g., place a product screenshot into a nice Pexels lifestyle photo using Canva or Photopea).
- Build a small brand asset folder with edited versions so you don’t start from scratch every time.
- For landing pages: Use one hero photo + mostly undraw illustrations to keep it light and fast.
- Test your page: Ask “Does this look like a professional brand or random stock?” If it looks random, increase customization.

### Quick Implementation Checklist
- [ ] Define brand colors, mood, and style rules.
- [ ] Create 8–12 core assets (hero variations + illustrations).
- [ ] Apply consistent editing style to all photos.
- [ ] Use undraw illustrations with your exact brand colors.
- [ ] Optimize all images for web.
- [ ] Review the full site for visual cohesion.
- [ ] Test on mobile.

You **don’t need real photos** to look professional. Many successful websites today run almost entirely on well-curated and customized free stock + illustrations. Focus heavily on **consistency and customization**, and your site will look intentional and premium.
