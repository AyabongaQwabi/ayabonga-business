# Competitor landscape (South Africa — software & technical leadership)

Research date: 2026-05-26. For **business.qwabi.co.za** positioning, comparison pages, and content gaps.

## Tier 1 — Large software agencies

| Competitor | Strength | Weakness vs Qwabi | Counter-message |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Entelect | Enterprise delivery, brand trust | Overhead, junior benches, slow MVPs | Direct senior builder; no PM layer |
| DVT | Microsoft/mobile scale | Minimum engagement size | Fixed-phase MVP, founder-facing |
| SovTech | High-volume custom apps | Black-box delivery | You own the codebase; talk to the engineer |
| Warp Development | Cape Town web/mobile | Agency rotation | TaaS: same senior through launch |
| Netgen / Specno / Bluegrass | Product studios | Team churn | Single accountable partner |

**Defend with:** `/vs/*`, buyer guides, case studies (ClinicPlus, Laundry, Queens Connect), developer hubs.

## Tier 2 — Fractional CTO / consultant

| Competitor | Strength | Weakness vs Qwabi | Counter-message |

## Tier 3 — Offshore / no-code

| Competitor | Strength | Weakness vs Qwabi | Counter-message |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Offshore shops | Low hourly rate | Timezone, quality lottery | SAST overlap, senior-only |
| No-code / AI-only MVPs | Fast demo | Scale ceiling | Custom when PMF proven |

**Defend with:** `/vs/senior-engineering-vs-no-code`, honest cost guides.

## What we do not compete on

- Cheapest hourly rate
- Largest team size
- Salary/job/course queries

## What we compete on

- Time to production MVP (typical 6–10 weeks scoped)
- Founder talks to the person writing code
- SA payment rails (Paystack, Stitch, Ozow) and POPIA-aware patterns
- Technical ownership without equity dilution
