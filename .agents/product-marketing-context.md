## Product overview

**One-liner:** Qwabi Engineering builds AI-powered software systems that help businesses automate, scale, and modernise their operations.

**What it does:** We design and ship custom software, platforms, mobile apps, APIs, and AI automation for South African businesses and agency partners. Delivery is engineering-led: discovery and architecture first, staged builds with visible milestones, then optional retainers for growth and support.

**Product category:** Custom software development company · AI-powered software systems · app and SaaS (Software as a Service) development South Africa

**Product type:** B2B project delivery + ongoing retainers + white-label delivery

**Business model:**

- **Projects:** Scoped builds in ZAR; sweet spot roughly R30k–R250k; larger systems R250k+
- **Minimum fit:** ~R10k–R15k (repel R2k “quick website” requests)
- **Retainers (central):** Build & Support · Product Growth · AI Systems
- **White-label:** Reliable execution for agencies (reliability, margin, delivery)

## Target audience

**Target companies:** Startups and SMMEs digitising or scaling; scaling operators needing automation; agencies needing a technical delivery partner.

**Decision-makers:** Founders, CEOs, operations leads, innovation managers, agency owners.

**Primary use case:** “We need a system that runs the business, not just a website.”

**Jobs to be done:**

- Replace manual/WhatsApp chaos with a real operational system
- Launch or rebuild a product with clear scope and milestones
- Integrate payments, APIs, and AI where it saves real work
- Keep one accountable technical owner after launch via retainer

**Ideal client profile (from Future Start):** Clear scope, good communication, respects timelines, values staged delivery.

**Worst-fit filter (from painful middleman patterns):** Scope instability, payment delays, discount pressure, unclear ownership, middlemen who cannot commit to decisions.

## Problems and pain points

**Core problem (customer language):** “We need a system, not just a website. Something that actually runs the business.”

**Why alternatives fall short:**

- Large enterprises and big consultancies: slow decisions, layers between buyer and builder
- Local web shops: websites and brochures, weak systems thinking
- Marketing-led agencies: pretty delivery, thin engineering ownership
- Cheap freelancers: broken handoffs, no architecture, no long-term ownership

**What it costs them:** Time, revenue leakage, reputational risk (especially for agencies), and restart cost on a bad build.

**Emotional tension:** “We have been burned before.” “We do not know exactly what we need.” “This has to work for real customers, not a demo.”

## Switching dynamics

**Push:** Failed or abandoned build, manual ops breaking, agency delays, payment flows that fail in production.

**Pull:** Clear engineering confidence (DVT-style clarity, more human), AI systems where they remove real work, staged risk.

**Habit:** Sunk cost on current vendor; fear of migration.

**Anxiety:** Price, trust, “can they handle our complexity?”

## Brand voice

**Tone:** Simple, relatable, natural, constructive, clear, calm, lightly confident.

**Style:** Outcomes first; short sentences; direct like WhatsApp, but polished. Explain like a business owner who codes. No corporate brochure voice.

**Personality:** Engineering clarity inspired by firms like DVT (clarity and confidence) but warmer, smaller, and closer to the work.

**Humor:** Light, occasional, dry/subtle. No politics, religion, or hustle preaching.

**Technical depth:** Benefits and outcomes in hero and sections; stack in lower sections or “Built with” blocks.

**AI positioning:** “AI-powered software systems” is core narrative. Do not oversell AI on every line. Capability, not identity.

**Pronouns:** **We** on this site only.

### WhatsApp voice benchmark (Ayabonga direct outreach)

Sound: direct, calm, helpful, technically capable, not salesy. Short sentences. Consultative. Frame around systems and problem-solving, not “we build websites.”

**Outbound (cold/warm intro), variation A:**

> Hi 👋🏾
>
> I had a look at your business and I think there’s an opportunity to simplify some of your operations digitally.
>
> What are you currently using to manage things right now?

**Inbound (they reached out first), variation B:**

> Hey 👋🏾
>
> Thanks for reaching out.
>
> Before I suggest anything, I just want to understand:
>
> - what you’re trying to improve,
> - what’s slowing things down,
> - and whether this is something that needs a website, automation, or a full system.

**Qualification (scope gather), variation C:**

> Hi 👋🏾
>
> I work mainly on custom software systems, automation and business platforms.
>
> Can you send me:
>
> - what the business does,
> - what process needs improvement,
> - and examples of anything similar you’ve seen?

**Rules:** No fluff intros. No hype. Emoji optional (👋🏾) but not required on every message. Follow up with milestones and written scope, not pressure.
